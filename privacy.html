Privacy Policy

This privacy policy only applies to the Pyxis: Notes app.

Pyxis: Notes only collects anonymous analytics. If you opt in to share information with developers, Apple provides this anonymous information to the developers.

Location

The app uses location services built into your device. Your location is only accessed when you provide permission to the app. Your location is never shared with us.

Third Party Services

The app collects anonymous analytics to measure which features are used the most. This allows the developer to focus on improving the functionality that matters to you.

  • The app uses TelemetryDeck as the tool to collect anonymous analytics. Their privacy policy is available at telemetrydeck.com/privacy.
  • The app uses RevenueCat as the service to capture analytics related to in-app purchases and subscriptions. Their privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Changes to This Policy

The privacy policy may be updated at any time. If you have any questions, please contact me via the Information/Settings section in the app or at pyxisnotes@gmail.com.
