Using Apple Shortcuts with Pyxis

Pyxis works great with Apple Shortcuts if you want to capture locations faster, open weather, or perform batch operations. This page shows practical shortcut setups to get you started.

Pyxis 3.5 releasing on April 27th!

Example Shortcuts

Add Location

You spot a place worth remembering and save it to Pyxis instantly. The shortcut captures the location, creates a new entry, and drops it into your map workflow without opening multiple screens.

Get Tag List

You want a quick overview of how your notes are organized. This shortcut fetches all current tags so you can scan your categories and decide what to explore or clean up next.

Find Nearby Notes

You are in a location and want context from your past. This shortcut looks around your current area and returns nearby notes, helping you reconnect with places, ideas, and memories tied to where you are now.

Rename Tag

Your organization system evolves over time. This shortcut updates an existing tag name so your notes stay consistent, searchable, and aligned with how you currently label your content.

Current Weather

You are planning what to do next and need conditions right now. This shortcut checks the weather for your current location and gives you a fast snapshot before you head out.
